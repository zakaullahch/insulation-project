Our Houston Roots & Mission

Why Standard Insulation Fails in the Houston Climate

Houston homes face some of the most aggressive environmental conditions in North America. Between subtropical humidity (averaging 75%–90%), scorching 100°F summer afternoons, and unventilated attics that regularly hit 145°F, ordinary fiberglass batts quickly compress, lose R-value, and allow hot attic air to penetrate living rooms.

We founded Insulation Contractor USA to solve this exact problem. Rather than just rolling out generic insulation, our certified building science technicians conduct full thermal leak detection, seal electrical and plumbing chases, and install high-density blown-in cellulose, spray foam, and injection foam engineered specifically for Climate Zone 2A.

01

Humidity & Mold Defense

Airtight ceiling barriers keep humid exterior air from condensing inside walls and creating dangerous mold spores.

02

HVAC Lifespan Protection

Lowering attic temperatures by 30°F prevents your AC ductwork from cooking and stops continuous compressor strain.

Scientific Precision

Our 4-Step Insulation Process

We eliminate guesswork with a documented, building-science protocol that guarantees maximum energy efficiency from attic to foundation.

01

Infrared Thermal Audit

We conduct an extensive attic and ceiling thermal inspection using infrared cameras to map out invisible insulation voids, heat leaks, and air bypasses.

02

HEPA Removal & Prep

When replacing old or pest-damaged insulation, our team vacuums debris directly outside into sealed containment bags, followed by hospital-grade sanitized wipe-down.

03

Precision Air Sealing

Before adding new insulation, we seal every ceiling gap, wiring drop, recessed light can, and attic hatch with expanding foam to stop stack-effect air movement.

04

High-Density Blow / Foam

We install premium blown-in or spray foam insulation to calibrated depth markers, verifying a continuous R-38 to R-60 blanket with zero settling gaps.
