COMMON QUESTIONS

What to Expect When You Contact Us

Is the on-site thermal insulation inspection really free?

Yes, 100% free with zero high-pressure sales. Our technician visits your home, inspects your attic floor, measure current R-values, uses a FLIR thermal camera to locate hidden drafts, and gives you a written, transparent estimate on the spot.

How quickly can you schedule an estimate or installation?

We provide same-day or next-day on-site estimates across Houston, Pasadena, The Woodlands, League City, and Baytown. Installations can typically be scheduled within 2 to 5 business days, with most residential jobs completed in a single day.

Do you assist with CenterPoint Energy rebate paperwork?

Yes! As a recognized regional efficiency contractor, our team handles all rebate verification, blower door pre/post documentation, and filing so that eligible discounts are applied directly to your project.
